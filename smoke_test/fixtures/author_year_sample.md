# Sample Paper

## End-to-End Setting

We use TACTICZERO (Wu et al. 2021a) to study embedding architectures in an end-to-end setting.
TACTICZERO learns using only RL and interaction with the ITP environment.

As mentioned in (Yang et al. 2023), problems such as compute requirements and private code have
made research in the area difficult. Benchmarks exist for HOL Light (Bansal et al. 2019a;
Kaliszyk, Chollet, and Szegedy 2017), HOL4 (Wu et al. 2021a), and Lean (Yang et al. 2023).
